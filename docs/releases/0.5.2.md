# Screepub 0.5.2

This release keeps more of the way your script looked on the page.

## Your script keeps more of its formatting

- **Underlined words arrive underlined.** Screenwriting apps draw an
  underline as artwork sitting under the text rather than as part of it, so
  Screepub used to lose them completely. It now finds them and keeps them.
- **Inserts and chyrons keep their own look.** A text message or a title
  card set in a different typeface or size used to arrive in the body
  typewriter font instead. It keeps its own look now. On by default: turn
  off **Keep the PDF's font shifts** in the reader's formatting rail for one
  typeface throughout.

## In the app

- **Check for Updates shows you what changed.** The release notes open in
  the app now, instead of sending you out to a web page.
- **A failed update check tells you why.** It used to say only that the
  check had failed, which left you nothing to act on. Now it says what
  happened and what to do about it.
- **Converting shows real progress.** The spinner used to sit there without
  moving until the whole script was done.

## Good to know

- An underline under part of a line may not come through. Screepub keeps an
  underline that runs beneath most of a line and skips a shorter one,
  because the alternative is underlining words your script did not.
- The typeface and size of an insert are kept for a whole paragraph at a
  time. A single word in a different font, mid-sentence, still arrives in
  the surrounding typeface.
