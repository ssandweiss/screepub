# Screepub 0.5.4

Three ways a script could arrive wrong, all found by reading real ones.

## Your script arrives whole

- **A song lyric could delete most of the book.** A lyric line's slash,
  landing next to italics, could open a comment that swallowed everything
  until the next one. On a 110-page script that removed 63%: 67 of 97
  scenes, with no warning at all. If a script has ever seemed to skip a
  stretch, convert it again.
- **Two characters talking at once stay two characters.** Simultaneous
  speech printed in side-by-side columns could arrive with both names
  demoted to ordinary text and the two speeches run together into a single
  sentence, so it read as though one person said both halves.

## Pages fill again

- **A long speech no longer pushes a whole page.** A speech that would not
  fit in what was left of a page used to move over in one piece, ending the
  page two thirds of the way down. It splits now, keeping the character
  name and the opening lines together the way a printed script does.

## Good to know

- That last change has been read on a Kindle, but not yet with a very long
  speech at a large font size. If a character name ever ends up alone at
  the foot of a page, that is the case worth reporting. <!-- caveat: registry-8b -->
