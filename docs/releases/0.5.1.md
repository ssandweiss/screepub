# Screepub 0.5.1

Dialogue that ran over a page used to break in the middle of a sentence.

## What's fixed

- **A speech split across a page reads as one speech again.** When a
  character's line ran past the bottom of a page in your PDF, the two
  halves arrived as separate paragraphs, so the book broke mid-sentence
  and the rest of the line started fresh underneath. They are joined back
  into the speech you wrote.
- **The PDF reader is up to date.** Screepub uses Mozilla's PDF software
  to read your script, and this release moves to a newer version of it.
  Nothing else about your converted scripts changes.

## Good to know

- Whether you ever saw the broken sentences depended on which app wrote
  your PDF. Final Draft and Highland print a small marker where a speech
  continues onto the next page, and Screepub already understood it. Celtx
  does not print one, so those scripts are where the breaks showed up.
