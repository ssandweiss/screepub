The heading names the cause, not the failure. "Scanned PDF, no text" tells you what to do next; "Something went wrong" does not. Alarm is used here and on untested device rows, nowhere else.

Three ways out, always. Convert anyway appears only for not-screenplay, because that is the one guard a reader can meaningfully overrule; the other codes describe a file the engine genuinely cannot read.
